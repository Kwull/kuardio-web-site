Not a medical device

Kuardio is a wellness logging app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease and does not give medical advice. The values come from your cuff. Reference categories are taken from published guidelines and are shown for information only. Always talk to your doctor about your blood pressure and your treatment, and don't change medication based on the app. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

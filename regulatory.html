Regulatory information

How Kuardio relates to the QardioArm's medical device clearances

Kuardio is a wellness logging app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. The measurement is made by the QardioArm cuff; Kuardio starts it, displays the values the cuff reports and stores them in Apple Health.

What Kuardio does

  • Connects to the user's own QardioArm over the standard Bluetooth SIG Blood Pressure Profile (service 0x1810, measurement characteristic 0x2A35) and sends the cuff's start and stop command.
  • Does not change the cuff, its firmware or its measurement algorithm. The cuff measures and computes the values.
  • Saves blood pressure and pulse to Apple Health, and shows the user's own history, trends and a report they can share with their clinician.
  • Shows optional reference categories, always attributed to their guideline (ESH 2023, ACC/AHA 2017 or NICE NG136). They can be turned off. Kuardio gives no alerts, advice or diagnosis. For very high values it only states the guideline range and suggests repeating the measurement and following the clinician's advice.

The FDA 510(k) summary for K140067 says the cuff "requires an external device (e.g. a smartphone) to display results" and its indications for use state that it "is not intended to be a diagnostic device". Kuardio is that display and logging part and makes no diagnostic claims.

How Kuardio uses the cuff: test report

Kuardio does not measure; it starts the cuff, receives the result over the standard Bluetooth Blood Pressure Service and stores it in Apple Health unchanged. The interoperability test report (PDF) documents the protocol, a logged session with a real cuff (every packet with raw bytes and decoded values), the automated tests that replay it, error handling and end-to-end value integrity. The raw session log is in the source repository.

User manuals

Qardio's websites are going offline. Archived copies of the official QardioArm user manual (instructions for use, safety information and cuff care) are available from the Internet Archive: 2020 edition. Always follow the manual's instructions for use and safety warnings.

Status of Qardio

Qardio's European companies are bankrupt or dissolved, and the official Qardio app is no longer on the App Store. That is why owners of a QardioArm need an independent app such as Kuardio, and why permission from Qardio can't be obtained.

Can the rights holder be reached? No. In the public report of 25 August 2026 the court-appointed trustee of Qardio Netherlands B.V. writes that Qardio's intellectual property was transferred for no consideration to the parent company, Qardio, Inc., and that the trustee "has repeatedly tried to get in touch with the parent company … unfortunately, to date this has led to nothing" (our translation; report 9, Dutch, PDF). The IP is listed with sale proceeds of € 0.00 and no interested parties.

The trademark. "QardioArm" is US trademark Reg. No. 4971993 of Qardio, Inc. (San Francisco). The USPTO record shows the attorney of record withdrew on 4 October 2024 and the 10-year renewal due by 7 June 2026 has not been filed (USPTO TSDR · record captured 22 October 2026, PDF). Kuardio uses the name only to say which cuff it works with, the referential use that trademark law allows for compatible products, and contains no Qardio code, firmware, logos, images or services.
